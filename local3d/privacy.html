PRIVACY POLICY

Local creation, clear privacy.

Last updated: September 2, 2026

Images and generated models

Local 3D processes selected photos, camera images, object selections and 3D generation directly on your device. Knoet Studio does not upload these images or generated models to a cloud generation service.

Generated models are stored locally on your device. Files leave the device only when you choose to share or export them through services or apps selected by you.

Camera and Photo Library

Camera access is used only when you choose to take a source image. Photo Library access is used only to let you select an image for 3D generation.

Advertising

Local 3D displays advertising provided by Google Mobile Ads. Google and its partners may process information such as device identifiers, IP address, advertising interactions, approximate location and diagnostic information to provide, secure and measure ads. This processing is governed by Google's privacy policies and your device or consent settings.

Read more in Google's Privacy Policy.

No accounts or generation credits

Local 3D does not require an account, cloud subscription or generation credits. The app does not receive your generated content in order to create a model.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact Knoet Studio at knoetcontact@gmail.com.
